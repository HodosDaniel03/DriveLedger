FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know about Odometer data sources.

How accurate are the cost estimates? expand_more

Estimates are built from millions of recorded repair orders, verified OEM service intervals, and country-specific parts distribution wholesale catalogs. We constantly calibrate labor rates with zip-level workshop survey data.

Where do the repair prices come from? expand_more

Data aggregates standard manufacturer labor guide units (flat rates) multiplied by certified independent workshop hourly billing ranges across the US, UK, and Europe.

Do I need to create an account? expand_more

No. All core calculations, OBD2 diagnosis reports, and ownership matrices are 100% free with no email or registration gate.

Which regions and currencies are supported? expand_more

We natively support United States (USD, Miles, Gallons), United Kingdom (GBP, Miles, MPG), and European Union member states (EUR, Kilometers, Liters).

How is the Ownership Value Score calculated? expand_more

It factors in residual depreciation curves, historical failure severity per 10,000 miles, accessibility of third-party replacement parts, and fuel consumption relative to its vehicle class.
