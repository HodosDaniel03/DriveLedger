P0420 Emissions & Exhaust
OBD-II GENERIC

Catalyst System Efficiency Below Threshold (Bank 1)

The engine control module (ECM) detects that the catalytic converter’s oxygen storage capacity on Bank 1 has degraded below acceptable EPA emissions thresholds.

Confirmed Symptoms
  • warning Check engine light (MIL) illuminated with no catastrophic loss of drivability.
  • air Noticeable sulfur or rotten-egg exhaust odor following extended highway loads.
  • cancel Immediate rejection during state or regional OBD-II tailpipe readiness checks.

Check before you replace

Avoid replacing a $1,200 catalytic converter prematurely. Verify these 3 live sensor PIDs:

Graph live oxygen sensor voltages Verified

Compare upstream PID O2S11 (should switch rapidly 0.1V–0.9V) against downstream PID O2S12 (should remain flat at ~0.45V). If downstream mirrors upstream cycling, converter washcoat is exhausted.

Inspect short & long term fuel trims Verified

Verify PIDs SHRTFT1 and LONGFT1. Combined trim corrections exceeding ±10% indicate an air-fuel metering fault contaminating the catalyst substrate.

Acoustic & smoke leak inspection Verified

Check for micro-fissures or pinholes along the manifold gasket and flex pipe within 12 inches of the upstream sensor bung before purchasing a replacement converter.
