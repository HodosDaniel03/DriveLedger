Catalyst System Efficiency Below Threshold (Bank 1)

The engine control module (ECM) detects that the catalytic converter's oxygen storage capacity on Bank 1 has degraded below acceptable EPA emissions thresholds.

Check before you replace

Avoid replacing a $1,200 catalytic converter prematurely. Verify these 3 live sensor PIDs:

1
Graph live oxygen sensor voltages

Compare upstream PID O2S11 (0.1V–0.9V switching) against downstream PID O2S12 (~0.45V steady). If downstream mirrors upstream, converter is exhausted.

2
Inspect short & long term fuel trims

Verify PIDs SHRTFT1 and LONGFT1. Combined trim exceeding ±10% indicates an air-fuel metering fault contaminating the catalyst.

3
Acoustic & smoke leak inspection

Check for micro-fissures or pinholes along the manifold gasket and flex pipe within 12 inches of upstream sensor before buying converter.
